      Words only one brand used

      The short list of words nobody else in the fifty has touched. Most distinctiveness lives here.

      Lines were collected from brand websites, press releases and annual reports, and reflect what each brand leads with as of September 2026. Where a brand has moved on, its earlier line is included and marked. Mission and purpose statements are marked where a brand leads with one instead of a tagline.

      Counting is by brand, not by line: a brand that uses a word twice still counts once. Close variants are grouped, so “building” counts with “build” and “engineered” with “engineering”.

      Taglines change quickly. If one here has moved on, the point probably still stands.
